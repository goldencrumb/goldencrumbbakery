Frequently Asked Questions

Are your millet cookies and snacks good for kids?

Absolutely! Our millet mini bites and healthy muffins for kids are the perfect after-school snack. We use 100% traditional millets and no refined sugar, making them the ultimate choice for guilt-free snacking.

Do you use eggs, maida, artificial colours, or preservatives?

Never. Our core promise is 100% eggless, no maida, no artificial colours, and no preservatives. Every millet brownie and cookie is baked fresh using natural ingredients and naturally sweetened alternatives, completely free from commercial lab chemicals.

Where do you deliver your healthy bakery items?

We bake fresh in our Manapakkam kitchen and offer delivery across Chennai and Tamil Nadu. We also accept Pan-India pre-orders! You can easily buy our eggless, healthy cookies online and get fresh baked guilt-free treats delivered directly to your doorstep.

How long do the millet brownies and treats stay fresh?

Because we proudly bake preservative free cookies, real food has a natural shelf life. We recommend enjoying your healthy millet snacks within 3 to 4 days of delivery for the best taste and texture.
